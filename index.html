Why Physoon

      
A Python runner with nothing between you and the interpreter

      
No accounts, no queued jobs on someone else's server, no watching a spinner while a container boots. Just an editor, an interpreter, and your code.

    

    

      

        01
        
Nothing to download

        
Physoon runs a real CPython runtime (Pyodide, compiled to WebAssembly) — not a look-alike interpreter — so the full standard library and many popular packages actually work. The runtime (~10-20MB) downloads once from a CDN and is cached by your browser after that; it needs an internet connection the first time.

      

      

        02
        
Nothing leaves your browser

        
Your code runs entirely inside this JavaScript interpreter, in this tab. There's no server storing or reading what you write.

      

      

        03
        
Tracebacks you can read

        
Errors print exactly as they would in a terminal, so you can debug the same way you already know how.

      

      

        04
        
Real Python 3, in your browser

        
Physoon runs an actual CPython runtime (via Pyodide/WebAssembly), so the entire standard library works — every module, generators, decorators, all of it — plus many popular packages like numpy and pandas. The first run downloads the Python runtime (~10-20MB, cached after that); every run after is instant.

Why this SQL sandbox

      
A real query engine with nothing between you and the database

    

    

      

        01
        
Nothing to download

        
Physoon runs a real SQLite database (compiled to WebAssembly) — not a look-alike engine — so full SQL works: joins, subqueries, unions, CASE expressions, transactions, all of it. The engine (~1-2MB) downloads once from a CDN and is cached by your browser after that.

      

      

        02
        
A sample database, ready to go

        
Students, courses, and enrollments are preloaded so you can practice JOINs and GROUP BY immediately — reset it any time.

      

      

        03
        
Errors you can read

        
Unknown tables, bad columns, and syntax mistakes come with a plain-English suggestion, not just a stack trace.

Privacy Policy

    
What we collect, why, and what you can do about it — in plain language.

  


  

    


      
What we collect

      
When you create an account, we collect your username, email address, and password (which we never see in plain text — it's securely handled by our authentication provider, Supabase). When you use Physoon, we also store the code and projects you write, your typing course progress, and your practice history (speed and accuracy scores), so that your work is there when you come back.

      
If you use Physoon without an account (as a guest), none of this is sent anywhere — it stays only in your own browser, and is lost if you clear your browser data.


      
How we use it

      
We use your information only to run Physoon: to log you in, to save and restore your projects and progress, and to let you reset your password if you forget it. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.


      
Who else sees it

      
Physoon is built on two third-party services that process data on our behalf:

      
    
        
  • Supabase — stores your account and project data, and handles login/authentication.
    • 
        
  • Vercel — hosts the website itself.
    • 
      

      
Both are reputable infrastructure providers used by many websites; we don't share your data with anyone beyond what's needed to run these services.


      
Cookies and local storage

      
Physoon uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in and to store guest-mode data. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.


      
Your rights

      
You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us (see below). You can also delete individual projects yourself at any time from within the app.


      
Children's privacy

      
Physoon is intended for learners of coding age and up. We don't knowingly collect data from young children without appropriate parental awareness.
